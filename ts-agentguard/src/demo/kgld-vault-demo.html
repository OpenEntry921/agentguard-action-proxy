Why This Matters

AI Agents can make financial decisions.

AgentGuard ensures that approved actions are the same actions that are executed.

It detects drift attacks, unauthorized vault access, and cumulative exposure violations before settlement.

AgentGuard evaluates not only individual transactions
but also cumulative exposure across multiple actions.

This prevents AI Agents from gradually increasing risk
through a series of seemingly safe transactions.
